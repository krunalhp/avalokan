### INT8 static quantization findings

1. **Naive full-graph opset-11 QDQ (`int8_naive_qdq`) fails completely on YOLOX:** YOLOX concatenates grid-space bounding box regression outputs (`0..52`) with objectness and class probabilities (`0..1`) into a single `(1, 3549, 85)` output tensor. Quantizing the final `Concat` node forces probabilities to share a `uint8` scale with box coordinates, crushing `frame0.jpg` max objectness from `0.9463` to `0.2021` and yielding **0 detections** above the `0.3` threshold.
2. **Opset-13 per-channel `Conv`-only QDQ (`int8_conv_qdq`) works well on YOLOX Tiny (`5.06M`, standard convolutions) but degrades YOLOX Nano (`0.91M`, depthwise separable convolutions):**
   - `yolox_tiny_int8_conv_qdq_t1` preserves **94.05% box recall** and **0.9304 mean IoU** against `yolox_tiny_fp32_t1` while reducing single-thread CPU cost by **40.2%** (`327.15` → `195.54 CPU-s/video-h` at 2 fps) and shrinking artifact size from `20.2 MB` to `5.3 MB`.
   - `yolox_nano_int8_conv_qdq_t1` drops to **25.60% box recall** (depthwise separable convolutions degrade under post-training quantization without QAT) and runs slower (`17.62 ms` vs `12.18 ms`) due to QDQ quantize/dequantize node overhead on small tensors.
